Starter dataset
From donor to patient

One year of a blood system: who donated, what was collected, where every unit went, which hospitals ran short, and what happened when the power went off. Every team gets the same data, so you can spend your 24 hours building.

This data is synthetic. It was generated by a model built on Kenyan geography and published reference figures. No real donor, patient or facility record is included, and the numbers do not describe any real hospital or blood centre.

How blood moves in the model

The system

  1. DrivePeople donate at schools, churches, mosques, workplaces and markets.
  2. TestScreening takes 1 to 6 days. Reactive donations are discarded.
  3. ProcessRegional centres make red cells, plasma and platelets, or send whole blood.
  4. DeliverRoutine runs to hospitals 1 to 3 times a week.
  5. RequestHospitals use their own stock first, then call for emergency supply.
  6. Expire or spoilRed cells last 42 days, platelets 5. Power cuts warm fridges.

Real systems are messy. So is this one, on purpose.

Known data problems

  • About 450 donors are registered twice under different IDs (same birth date, sex, county and phone last 4 digits).
  • About 1.2% of donor records have no blood group.
  • 14% of requests have the hospital name typed differently. Use facility_id as the truth.
  • 5% of emergency requests record the patient's group as unknown.
  • About 3% of transfers were never confirmed as received.
  • Temperature loggers drop out for hours, more often on 2G and 3G sites. 60 readings are sensor glitches (-99.0, 99.9, 0.0).
  • Some donors gave blood again before the minimum gap of 3 months for men and 4 for women.
Getting started

First query

import pandas as pd

req = pd.read_csv("requests.csv",
    parse_dates=["requested_at"])
unmet = req[req.outcome != "fulfilled"]
unmet.groupby("patient_blood_group").size()
-- SQL (load the CSVs into SQLite, DuckDB or Postgres)
-- where does O negative run out?
SELECT facility_id, COUNT(*) AS unmet
FROM requests
WHERE patient_blood_group = 'O-'
  AND outcome <> 'fulfilled'
GROUP BY facility_id
ORDER BY unmet DESC;

Times are East Africa Time. Units collected in September and October 2025 that were still on the shelf on 1 November are included and marked opening_stock. gen.py in the download rebuilds everything, including a ready-made SQLite database; change the seed for a fresh version.

Rules

Using the data

  • Use it freely in your build and demo. Say in your submission that you used the starter dataset.
  • Do not present findings from it as facts about real Kenyan facilities.
  • You may add real data. Cite where it came from.
  • AI coding tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Cursor and similar) are allowed. Say which ones you used and for what in your README. Every line you submit must be written during the 24 hours, and your team must be able to explain it.

Sources for the model's figures

  • Infection rates among donors (HBV 3.5%, HCV 3.2%, syphilis 1.6%, HIV 1.2%): study of voluntary donors in Homa Bay, Kisumu and Siaya
  • National collection gap, staffing and cold chain gaps: Daily Nation report on blood transfusion centres
  • Blood group mix: approximation from Kenyan donor studies and KNBTS comments in The Standard
  • Shelf lives follow standard practice. Seasonality follows the Kenyan school calendar, holiday road traffic and malaria seasons.
